# Proof and evidence

**Mandatory** for claims with numbers or named clients. Sources: `02_MESSAGING_V17.md`, `06_PROOF_INDEX.md`.

## Rules

- Every number traces to the Proof Library, messaging verified figures, or a named source in the brief.
- Named clients require logo clearance; otherwise use a credibility descriptor.
- Thin industry coverage (Energy & Utilities, Construction, Agriculture, Airline, Commercial Real Estate, Government, Consumer Tech): flag before promising proof — often a single story cannot carry a whole sequence.
- Base URL for success-story slugs: `https://www.66degrees.com/success-stories/`

## Verified messaging figures (non-exhaustive)

See `messaging_and_terminology.md` for Wood Mackenzie, Press Ganey, Schreiber, GFS, and portfolio credentials.

## Tier guidance from proof index

1. **Named clients** — strongest when cleared (e.g. Six Flags, PGA of America, Gordon Food Service, WEX, …).
2. **Hard numbers** — homepage and story figures (e.g. $10M annual savings Fortune 250; 50–70% touchpoints automated; Pet Food Experts 80%/60% cost reductions). Use only when the figure matches the cited context.
3. **Credentials** — Diamond Partner; 4x Partner of the Year; Everest PEAK 2025 Leader and Star Performer; CRN / ISG / IDC recognitions as listed in source.

Do not invent figures to fill a gap. Prefer fewer true numbers over many decorative ones.
