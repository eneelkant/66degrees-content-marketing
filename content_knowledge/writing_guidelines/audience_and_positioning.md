# Audience and positioning

**Mandatory.** Sources: `02_MESSAGING_V17.md`, `03_WRITING_RULES.md`, `00_README.md` brief fields.

## Primary audiences

- CxO and VP business decision makers (majority of copy)
- CIO, CTO, CDO, VP of data and IT (reader-journey persona)
- Industry and region as supplied in the brief

## Positioning

- Category: full-stack AI-native engineering across cloud, data and AI — between pure-play AI natives and traditional firms.
- Outcome framing: The AI-Native Enterprise (client outcome); Agentic describes machinery.
- Delivery: FDEs + global engineering + Paradigm° as one system.

## Brief requirements (ask before writing if missing)

1. Asset type
2. Reader (role, industry, region)
3. Purpose (the one thing the reader should do or believe)
4. Source material (event brief, success story, facts in hand)
5. CTA

## Alignment checks

- Content relevant to stated role / industry / region
- One purpose, one CTA
- Outcome first; do not restate the reader's problem
- Winner-side framing only
