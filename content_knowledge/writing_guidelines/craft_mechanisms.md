# Craft mechanisms (reference only)

Distilled from `08_CRAFT_*.md` (Sugarman, Halbert/Boron, Cialdini, persuasive copywriting, email mechanisms). **Reference only.** Where craft conflicts with 66 company facts, messaging, writing rules, or specs — **66 rules win**.

## Use in generation

Load a short reminder for long-form or persuasion-heavy assets:

- Slippery slide: each sentence earns the next; kill dead sentences.
- Trigger feature: one true early cue (partner tier, industry match, verified outcome).
- Harmony through yes: never fail the sentence-level credibility audit.
- Anticipate the next question in reader order.
- Emotion first for desire; logic for committee defense — but 66 winner-side emotion only.
- One offer, one decided next step; honest urgency only.
- Email: proof in subject paid off in line 1; introduce people through the figure they own; no fake scarcity.

## Do not import from craft

- Fear, guilt, shouty closes, disguised mail, invented stories/details, jargon-as-trust theater, consumer-style guarantees, or desperation framing.
