# Prohibited claims and patterns

**Mandatory.** Sources: `01_COMPANY_FACTS.md`, `02_MESSAGING_V17.md`, `03_WRITING_RULES.md`, `09_SPECS_AND_GUARDRAILS.md`.

## Hard prohibited (rewrite)

- Calling 66degrees a Google Cloud **Premier** Partner (must be Diamond)
- "66degrees builds AI for the enterprise" / "we build AI native enterprises"
- Quoting headcount
- "AI in 2026" as award shorthand
- ChrysalisOS; "The Agentic Enterprise"; PRISM without translating to Paradigm°
- Competitor clouds: AWS, Azure
- Messaging hard bans listed in `messaging_and_terminology.md`
- Invented logistics, speakers, agenda items, attendance, or social proof
- Unsupported numerical / performance / customer claims not in the brief or Proof Library
- Revenue figures presented as public achieved results
- Projected figures presented as achieved
- Fear, pressure, manufactured scarcity, pain selling
- Standalone credential brags ("We are a Diamond Partner")

## Soft warnings (style)

- Mechanical / AI-sounding triad patterns and summarising abstractions
- Recap bridges across a sequence
- Multiple credentials in one asset
- Generic consultant prompts
- Capitalizing "AI-Native Advantage" as if it were a program name
