# Reader journey (condensed)

**Recommendation / craft adapted to 66.** Source: `05_READER_JOURNEY.md`. Where this conflicts with company facts, messaging, or writing rules, those win.

## Engine (one paragraph)

Select a reader who already wants the outcome; pass their sorting reflex with an honest cue; buy the first second with a true relevant line; keep every sentence cheaper than quitting; make them feel seen; back every cue with inspectable substance; let them picture the outcome as their own; make one small fully specified step feel natural; give them words to defend it to their committee.

Ethical line: a cue is legitimate only when the substance it signals is real.

## Stages (writer checklist)

| # | Stage | Silent question |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Select | Who already wants this? |
| 1 | Sort | Is this for me, or is someone selling to me? |
| 2 | Stop | Reason to spend three seconds? |
| 3 | Enter | Is the first line worth finishing? |
| 4 | Slide | Is the next sentence worth more than leaving? |
| 5 | Relate | Do they understand my world? |
| 6 | Believe | Is this true and checkable? |
| 7 | Want | Can I see this working for me? |
| 8 | Act | What exactly do I do? |
| 9 | Defend | Can I justify this to my committee? |

## 66 resolutions of craft disagreements

- Hunger as **ambition with a clock**, not distress pressed for fear.
- **Plain wins over disguised** (no fake Re: subjects).
- Front-load value; withhold mechanism; grabbers must be facts.
- Unsolicited: first sentence short and true, start of argument not pitch. Solicited: lead with the answer.
- Momentum from substance; 66 register forbids staccato direct-response tics.
- Accuracy over warmth in compliments.
- Every number from Proof Library; no invented stories.
- Winner-side professional emotion only.
- Calm true constraints; never unauthorized urgency.
