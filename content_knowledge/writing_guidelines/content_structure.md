# Content structure

**Mandatory** where the format applies. Sources: `04_FORMAT_RULES.md`, `09_SPECS_AND_GUARDRAILS.md`.

## Universal guardrails

- One reader, one purpose, one CTA.
- Outcome first. Never restate the reader's problem.
- One 66 credential at most, embedded.
- Each point once. No recap bridges.
- Every fact from the brief, Proof Library, or live partner page.
- Deliver only the assets the brief lists.

## Event invite emails

Parts: subject, banner (event name, date, time, venue), title, body, CTA. **No preview text.**

- Subject: plainly an invitation, short, under 40 characters, no job titles, no clever wordplay.
- Title: maximum 12 words (never beyond 14), standing alone.
- Body opens: "On [date], we are hosting [what], and we would like you there", then why, then what they get. Never repeat logistics or the subject.
- Every invite stands alone. Each adds one new element. Last invite: last chance, what, where, when.
- Seats decline across the sequence without exact counts.
- Sign off: "Best regards, 66degrees"

## Registration pages (66 hosted)

1. Hero — H1, date, time, location, logos, one CTA (2–3 words)
2. Intro — H2 + 2–3 short paragraphs (~120 words); outcome first; dated CTA
3. Who should attend — 3–5 bullets, "Bold lead: Elaboration" (<25 words elaboration); specific job functions
4. Closing paragraph — 2–3 sentences, no header; one embedded credential
5. Agenda — time ("5:30 PM"), sentence-case title, one sentence on what happens / what guest gets
6. Speakers — real speakers only
7. Form — first name, last name, business email, company, consent (fixed)

Partner-hosted pages: partner live page verbatim; 66 adds only block 4.

## Leadership / external

Press, media, executive quotes, website, anything the CEO reads: follow `ceo_voice.md`.
