# Social and email

**Mandatory** for those channels. Sources: `04_FORMAT_RULES.md`, `09_SPECS_AND_GUARDRAILS.md`, `08_CRAFT_EMAIL_MECHANISMS.md` (craft adapted; 66 rules win).

## Event invite emails (limits)

| Element | Limit |
|---|---|
| Subject | Under 40 characters. Value in the first three words. Plainly an invitation. No job titles. |
| Preview text | None |
| Title | 12 words max (never beyond 14) |
| Body, invite 1 | About 90 words |
| Body, later invites | About 12% fewer words than previous; urgency up about 30% |
| CTA | 2 to 5 words; never repeated within a sequence |
| Sign off | "Best regards, 66degrees" |

Body order: `Hi {{FirstName}},` → "On [date], we are hosting…" → why → what they get → CTA.

## Social and field events (dinners, networking, outings)

Talk only about the venue, ambience, and how well spent the evening will be. Never the business reason for hosting. At most one light optional line that a host is available to talk through a project. Do not write "66degrees is hosting"; the sender already is 66degrees.

## Social posts

Match the existing approved template for that post type (announcement, speaker, final countdown, last chance): same character and word counts as the previous approved post of that type. Captions separate from in-design text. Do not invent character limits when no approved template is in the brief.
