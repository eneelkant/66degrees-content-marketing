## Every format

- One reader, one purpose, one CTA
- Outcome first; never restate the problem
- One embedded 66 credential at most
- Each point once; no recap bridges
- Facts only from brief / Proof Library / live partner page
- No dashes; no banned terms; no competitor clouds
- No fear, pressure or theatrics
- Sentence case for headings, subjects, titles, CTAs
- Deliver only assets the brief lists
