# Messaging and terminology

**Mandatory** brand messaging. Source: `02_MESSAGING_V17.md` (owner: Tommy Bliven).

## Core spine

- Tagline: "Engineering Your AI-Native Advantage."
- Mission: "turn AI ambition into an operating reality."
- Core promise: "Transforming Complexity into Clarity."
- Category claim: full stack AI-native engineering across cloud, data and AI services; the ground between pure-play AI natives and traditional firms, held by neither.

## Five value pillars (describe as lifecycle phases, never catalog names)

1. Turn AI Ambition into Advantage — Agentic Labs (strategy, workflow design)
2. Build the Foundation — AI-Native Foundations (through Paradigm Data)
3. Engineer AI Workflows — AI Workflow Transformation
4. Drive Adoption — AI Workforce Enablement
5. Optimize and Scale — Managed Agentic Services

## Delivery model

Forward deployed engineers (FDEs), global engineering teams and Paradigm° work as one system. FDEs work **alongside and with** client teams, never "embedded in" or "inside". FDEs define what to build and why; global engineering builds it. People act; platforms equip.

## Voice

- We are / we are not: In production, not in PowerPoint. Builders, not outside advisors. Specialists, not generalists. Convicted, not hedging. Human led: humans lead, agents execute. Governed, not reckless. Plain spoken, not jargon drunk.
- Core voice: Strategic, Precise, Approachable, Empathetic. Register: McKinsey and Deloitte.
- Outcomes always carry a number the client would recognize (from the Proof Library).

## Hard bans (terminology)

Do not use:

- pairing "paradigm" with "shift"
- "built on" + Google Cloud as a refrain (say "a Google Cloud partner" once)
- "automate the past"
- "The Agentic Enterprise"
- "AI-native consultancy" (say company)
- "Agent Garden" (say Industry Agent Libraries)
- "embedded" or "inside" for FDEs
- "daily use" (say adoption)
- "sandbox" as a positive
- "Sustain" as a phase
- "Build, Adopt, Evolve" as a named framework
- competitor fingerprints: powerful ally, speed to value, cutting edge, seamless, world class, purpose built, toolkit (as prose), unlock, journey, destination, finish line, "We do this by", own the outcome, "Architecting the AI-Native Enterprise"
- "AI-Native Advantage" capitalized as a program name (lowercase "your AI-native advantage" is fine)
- competitor clouds (AWS, Azure)
- Google Cloud Premier Partner / Premier Partner (use Diamond Partner)
